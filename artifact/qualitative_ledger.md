## CONSTRUCT examples (Figs. S1–S2)

Brushed steel / cast plate / rolled sheet severe-defect panels and the
single-image inference overlays are pre-existing gray-128 assets from
the CONSTRUCT PGDN run (seed 42); input and mask paths follow the
`data/construct/<surface>/` layout with basenames visible in the
panels.

**Limitation.** The pre-existing panels predate this ledger; for those,
image identity is recorded at the basename level only (the defect-type
folder was not captured), and the two VisA normal-case examples lack
exact split paths. Future regenerations record full dataset-relative
paths. No panel is used for quantitative claims.

## Matched-component atlas and spatial probe

The new main defect-localization figure uses independently exported arrays
from `artifact/defect_analysis_v1/maps/*_atlas.npz`, not slices of the
historical gallery. Its selected image identities are recorded in that
directory's `protocol.json`. The spatial figure uses `synthetic_probes.npz`
and the paired background summaries. Its stored `p0_minus_p1` arrays mean
before minus after; the figure builder negates them to display the defined
after-minus-before score change. No source-array values are rewritten.
